Gas safety

Gas work is regulated, and gas safety is not something to leave to chance. This website is for enquiries only, not emergencies.

If you smell gas or suspect a leak

  • Do not use switches, naked flames or anything that could cause a spark.
  • Open doors and windows and turn off the gas at the meter if it is safe to do so.
  • Call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 (free, 24 hours).
